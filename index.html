Choose your ticket paper

Pick the sheet that matches your paper. The drawings are to scale. Not sure? Count the tickets on one sheet and search for that number, e.g. "30".

Measure one ticket with a ruler, count the tickets across and down, and measure from the top and left edge of the sheet to the first ticket. The gaps between tickets are worked out for you.
